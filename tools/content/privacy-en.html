Privacy policy

Last updated · November 21, 2026

Surebet Stake Calculator
Developer · Iaroslav Beldin · beldin_dev@icloud.com

When you use the calculator

You enter decimal odds and stake amounts. The app calculates the result on your device and works without an account. A calculation event records the selected mode, the number of outcomes, the calculated return percentage and whether it was positive. The event does not include the odds or individual stakes you entered.

Usage statistics

I use Google Analytics for Firebase and AppMetrica to understand how people use the app. Examples include opening a section, using the calculator and completing the introduction. Events may include the app version, interface language, appearance, and identifiers for the installation and session. The app generates a random installation identifier so that activity from the same installation can be recognized over time.

The services also receive technical information such as device model, operating system, screen characteristics, session starts and network connection details. An IP address is visible to a service when the app connects and can be used to estimate your country, region or city. The app does not ask for GPS access. AppMetrica’s option to mask full IP addresses for EU users is turned off.

Crash reports

I use Firebase Crashlytics to investigate crashes and other errors. Reports may include the device model, operating system, app version, a stack trace showing where the error occurred, installation identifiers and context such as the section or calculator mode in use.

Optional tips

In versions that offer tips, Apple processes the purchase. To check whether purchases work, the app records the tip option and the result, such as completion, cancellation or failure. Some events include the price and currency. Analytics may also include the number of tips and the largest tip option purchased. Where Apple makes them available, AppMetrica can also process a transaction identifier and App Store receipt data automatically. I do not receive your payment card details.

Email support

You choose what to send to beldin_dev@icloud.com. I receive the sender address, message and any attachments through iCloud Mail. I use the correspondence to respond and handle follow-up questions. The app may record whether it could open your email app. That event does not include the contents of your email.

This website

GitHub hosts these pages and logs visitor IP addresses for security, including visits to this policy. The site itself does not add an analytics script, contact form or cookies. About GitHub Pages.

Service providers and safeguards

Google and AppMetrica process the data described above under terms that cover confidentiality, security measures and deletion. See the Firebase terms, Google Analytics terms and AppMetrica terms.

How long data is kept

The Google Analytics property is configured to retain event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months. New activity resets the user-level period. Standard aggregated reports are outside those settings. Google Analytics data retention.

For Crashlytics, Google keeps crash stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning to remove them from live and backup systems. Firebase privacy and security.

AppMetrica processes personal data for the duration of the service and the additional period needed to delete it in accordance with applicable law. AppMetrica data processing terms.

I keep support correspondence while I need it to handle the issue and possible follow-up questions. I delete it on request unless I am legally required to keep it.

Your choices

For questions or to request access, correction or deletion, email beldin_dev@icloud.com. You can also use that address to object to processing or withdraw consent where applicable. The app has no in-app analytics switch. An email request does not itself turn off the SDKs on your device.

For a request about email correspondence, writing from the original address helps me find it. Usage events are stored under technical identifiers, so an email address alone may be insufficient to locate a particular installation’s records. I will explain what I can identify and what action I can take.
