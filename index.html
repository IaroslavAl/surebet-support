Enter the odds and a total stake. See how much to allocate to each outcome and what each result would return.

Work through a calculation

With two outcomes at decimal odds of 2.10 and a total of 1,000, the calculator divides the stake equally.

Total stake1,000
Per outcome500
Calculated profit+50

A winning stake of 500 pays 1,050. Subtract the total stake of 1,000 to get a profit of 50, or a 5% return.

Ways to use the calculator

Distribute a total stake

Enter decimal odds for 2–20 outcomes and the total amount to allocate. Read the stake and result for each outcome.

Keep one stake fixed

Enter the amount for one outcome. The calculator works out the stakes for the remaining outcomes and the total required.

Compare your own stakes

Enter the stakes yourself and compare the profit or loss for each possible outcome.
